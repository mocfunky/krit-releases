Built for groups that play

What Krit brings together

01

Chat, voice, and video

Persistent text channels, private messages, LiveKit voice, webcams, screen sharing, replies, reactions, attachments, search, and rich formatting.

02

Game-night tools

Campaign Desk, Game Lobby, Community Atlas, scheduled sessions, characters, capacity, ready state, waitlists, and temporary voice spaces.

03

Watch and listen together

Twitch Watch Parties, Twitch chat, Spotify listening status, in-app Premium playback, and opt-in game activity.

04

Real community control

Hierarchical roles, 36 granular permissions, channel overrides, server grouping, rich rules, webhooks, moderation, and a three-level Control Center.

05

Privacy and safety

Verified email, optional 2FA, private attachment delivery, adult-media policy controls, quarantine scanning, blocking, reports, and session revocation.

06

Native app experience

Desktop auto-update, persistent login, media permissions, native window controls, unread badges, Android navigation, and responsive mobile layouts.
